LOTTO/1

A verifiable lottery. Selection, ranking, groups and proportional allocation, drawn from the drand quicknet beacon. Everything runs in this page — there is no server and nothing to trust but SHA-256.

1 Lottery

A participant's chance is their weight divided by the total weight. A weight of 0 means they cannot be drawn.

Paste a code, or a proof file. The beacon is fetched live from three drand relays; you can also paste one in the second box to check everything completely offline.
